# Landscape, lighting and native column-foot review

Kyle requested a beauty pass across all four regions, with direct image inspection,
parallel implementation, independent review and visible samples. His later column
closeup exposed an actual cropped source image that the earlier grounding checks missed.

For example, both Bloom broken-column sources were still29px wide on the last row of
48×56 cells. Accurate collision and moss could not make that shaft look complete. The
selected repair keeps the original upper40rows and registers native PixelLab footer pixels
into the lower14rows, leaving two transparent rows under a closed beveled footing.
The broader37px support then required a new title placement and separate fallen drum.
